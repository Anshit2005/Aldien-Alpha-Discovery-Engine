Read the validation columns carefully

Raw validation ICIR describes the validation association’s own sign. Direction-adjusted validation ICIR asks whether the training relationship persisted. The “Train − validation |ICIR|” column is an absolute-magnitude difference and ignores sign; a negative value does not establish stable generalization.

8 · What the experiment tells us

A defensible conclusion about the baseline stage—without claiming a validated trading strategy.

1 · Optimization

Evolutionary Search improves mean top-10 training fitness relative to random search in all five seeds.

2 · Exploitation

Evolution’s top formulas cluster structurally, indicating concentrated search and possible premature convergence.

3 · Exploration

Random Search samples a broader mix of expression structures; diversity is not equivalent to predictive quality.

4 · Generalization

Direction-adjusted validation is negative for both methods on average; evolution does not show a validation advantage.

5 · Next stage

Test whether LLM-guided semantic variation can add meaningful diversity while retaining fitness-driven selection.

Semantic exploration plus selection

In this matched-budget five-seed experiment, evolutionary search more consistently concentrates on higher-scoring training formulas, while random search retains broader operator diversity. The direction-adjusted validation results show that training relationships are not stable enough to establish an out-of-sample edge. The next experiment asks whether an LLM can introduce semantically meaningful exploration without losing evolutionary exploitation.

Interpretation and limitations

Use these qualifications when presenting the charts.

What is controlled

Same dataset SHA-256, train/validation splits, 20-day horizon, expanded seed pool, maximum depth 8, top-10 finalist count, and per-seed unique evaluation budget. Validation did not select the candidates.

What is not established

Statistical significance after multiple testing, robustness across market regimes, net returns after turnover/transaction costs, or a live-tradable strategy. Test and holdout were not used in this comparison.

Seed 67

All ten evolutionary finalists have zero valid validation IC days (reported coverage 0.0). The report shows validation fields as N/A and excludes the run from validation-agreement averages.

Diversity definition

Jaccard distance compares sets of DSL operators in formulas. It is not pairwise alpha-value correlation, economic diversification, or a direct measurement of strategy risk.
